| 1 | Parliament (procedure, privileges, committees) | 29+ Qs, every single year |
| 2 | President (+ VP, pardoning, ordinance) | 22 Qs, trap-heavy |
| 3 | Bill → Act legislative process | 19 Qs, Sutra for Mains GS-2 too |
| 4 | Schedules + Amendments | 17 + 16 Qs, factual scoring zone |
| 5 | Governor, key Articles, Supreme Court | Governor–Centre friction = Mains gold |
| 6 | FR vs DPSP vs Fundamental Duties, Preamble | Conceptual core, Mains GS-2 staple |
| 7 | 73rd/74th local governance, Emergency, EC/FC/CAG/UPSC | Steady 1–2 Qs each yearly |
