# BPSC Polity — 10-Day Crash Plan
## Priority-first · Laxmikanth + NCERT + PYQ

**Goal:** Cover the full BPSC Polity syllabus in 10 days, **highest-weight topics first**, using only Laxmikanth + NCERTs + the 115 tagged PYQs.

**Daily time:** 5–6 hours (minimum 4.5 if short on time)  
**Order logic:** PYQ frequency rank → finish ~70% of marks potential by Day 5 → mop up medium/low + full revision Days 6–10.
